# Disputes as weak labels

Date: 2025-09-23
Who: Marc Gagnon, Jules Bernard
Uncertainty: U1 Drift without fraud labels

## Plan (written before the run)

Hypothesis: Cardholder disputes (days, not months) confirm or clear a score-shape alarm.
What changes: Alarms checked against dispute counts 2 to 5 days later.
Setup: Dispute feed from 2 pilot processors.

## Result (written the same day)

Result: Disputes confirmed 2 of 3 waves; too few disputes at the smaller processor to say.
Learned: Works where volume is high. Small processors need pooled data, which raises privacy questions.
Outcome: Inconclusive
