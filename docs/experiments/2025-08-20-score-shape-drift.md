# Score-shape drift

Date: 2025-08-20
Who: Marc Gagnon, Priya Raman
Uncertainty: U1 Drift without fraud labels

## Plan (written before the run)

Hypothesis: Watching the shape of the model's own scores catches waves earlier than inputs do.
What changes: Drift measured on the score distribution's upper tail, not inputs.
Setup: Same 12 months plus the new summer wave.

## Result (written the same day)

Result: Caught all 3 waves within 1 day; 4 false alarms, all during merchant promotions.
Learned: The model's scores move before inputs do. Promotions are the last source of noise.
Outcome: Partly worked
